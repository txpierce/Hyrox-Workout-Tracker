1. Strength & Sled Days

Hypertrophy & Power
  • Pre-Workout (60m): 40-50g fast carbs (oatmeal/banana) + 20g whey protein.
  • Intra-Workout: Electrolytes + 5g BCAA / Essential Amino Acids.
  • Post-Workout (30m): 40g whey protein + 60g simple carbs (rice cereal/fruit juice) for muscle repair.
  • Protein Target: Maintain 1.0–1.2g per lb of body weight daily.

2. Run & Interval Days

Glycogen Focus
  • Pre-Run (90m): Low-fiber, high-carbs (white toast with honey, bagel).
  • During Long Runs (60m+): 30-60g carbs/hr via hydrogel energy gels + sodium electrolytes.
  • Post-Run: Immediate carbohydrate replenishment (1g/kg) to restore glycogen stores.
  • Hydration Rule: 500ml water with electrolyte tablets 2 hours before running.

3. Race & Simulation Days

Peak Performance
  • Carb-Load Phase (48h Pre): Increase carbs to 7-9g per kg body weight; reduce fat & fiber.
  • Race Morning (3h Pre): 100g easy-digesting carbs (white rice, banana, applesauce). Avoid fats.
  • Pre-Race (45m Pre): 200mg Caffeine + 1 Hydrogel (25g carbs).
  • Mid-Race Strategy: 1 energy gel between Station 4 (Sled Pull) and Station 5 (Rowing).

4. Rest & Recovery Days

Tissue Regeneration
  • Caloric Intake: Moderate decrease in carbs; maintain high protein for repair.
  • Healthy Fats: Increase Omega-3 fatty acids (salmon, walnuts, avocado) to reduce systemic inflammation.
  • Nighttime Protocol: 400mg Magnesium Glycinate + Tart Cherry Juice for deep REM sleep recovery.
  • Hydration: 3-4 liters of water throughout the day.
