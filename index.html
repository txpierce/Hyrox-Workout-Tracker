1. Strength & Sled Overload Days
  • High Protein (1.0–1.2g/lb): Focus on leucine-rich protein (chicken, beef, whey) post-session for muscle repair.
  • Targeted Carbs: Consume 40–60g carbs 60m pre-workout to fuel heavy sled drives and lunges.
  • Intra-Workout: Electrolytes + 5g BCAAs/EAAs during heavy lifting sessions over 75 mins.
2. Run & Aerobic Interval Days
  • High Carbs (2.5–3.5g/lb): Maximize glycogen storage in legs before track & long aerobic runs.
  • Pre-Run Fuel: Low-fiber, easily digestible carbs (oatmeal with banana or rice cakes with honey).
  • Intra-Run Hydration: 500–750ml water per hour with 500mg sodium and 30g carbs/hr for runs > 60m.
3. Race & Full Simulation Days
  • 48-Hour Carb Loading: Scale carbs to 3.5–4.5g/lb 2 days prior to race day while reducing fats.
  • Race Morning (3h prior): White rice with applesauce or bagel with jam; avoid fats & heavy dairy.
  • In-Race Gelling: 1 Hydro-gel at minute 30 (before Sled Pull/BBJ) and minute 55 (before Rowing).
  • Immediate Recovery: 4:1 Carb-to-Protein shake immediately after crossing the finish line.
4. Rest & Active Recovery Days
  • Maintenance Calories: Lower carbohydrate intake slightly while keeping protein high (1.0g/lb).
  • Healthy Fats (0.4–0.5g/lb): Avocado, extra virgin olive oil, and salmon to regulate hormonal balance.
  • Anti-Inflammatory Stack: Tart cherry juice, 2g Omega-3 fish oil, and 400mg Magnesium glycinate before bed.
