TRAINING \& RACE DAY FUELING PLAN

Carbohydrate, Protein \& Fat distribution tailored for compromise running stamina.

Target Calories 2,850 kcal High Energy Output
Carbohydrates 350g 50% Total Energy
Protein 180g 1.0g per lb BW
Fats 75g Hormonal \& Joint Support

1. Strength \& Heavy Sled Days

Glycogen Loading
  • Pre-Workout (60-90m): 50g fast-acting carbs (oatmeal + banana or bagel) + 25g whey protein.
  • Intra-Workout: 30g intra-carbs (hydrogel or cluster dextrin) + 500mg sodium electrolytes for sled push drive.
  • Post-Workout (Within 45m): 40g whey protein + 60g simple carbs (rice cereal or fruit juice) to accelerate muscle repair.

2. Compromised Run Days

Stamina Focus
  • Pre-Run (2h Prior): Low-fiber, high-carb meal (white rice + lean chicken breast or applesauce).
  • Hydration Strategy: Drink 16-20oz water with 1 electrolyte tablet 2 hours before starting run intervals.
  • Intra-Run (Runs > 45m): 1 energy gel (25g carbs) every 30 minutes to maintain steady blood glucose.

3. Full Race Sim \& Race Day

Peak Performance
  • 48 Hours Pre-Race: Increase daily carbohydrate intake to 7-8g per kg body weight; reduce fats \& heavy fiber.
  • 3 Hours Pre-Race: 75-100g simple carbs (white toast with honey + banana). Avoid high fats or slow-digesting protein.
  • 45 Minutes Pre-Race: 200mg Caffeine + 1 Hydrogel gel + 300ml sodium electrolyte water.

4. Active Recovery \& Rest Days

Tissue Repair
  • Caloric Intake: Reduce carbohydrate intake to baseline; maintain high protein (1.0-1.1g per lb) for muscle repair.
  • Healthy Fats: Increase Omega-3 fatty acids (salmon, walnuts, avocado) to reduce systemic inflammation.
  • Nighttime Protocol: 400mg Magnesium Glycinate + Tart Cherry juice 30 minutes before sleep to optimize REM recovery.
