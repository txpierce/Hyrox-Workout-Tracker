1. Pre-Workout Intra-Session Fueling

Consume 40–60g fast-acting carbs (banana, oats) 60 mins before high-intensity sled sessions. Sip 30g intra-workout cluster dextrin + 500mg sodium during sessions over 75 mins.

2. Race-Day Carbo-Loading Protocol

36 hours prior to race start, increase carb intake to 7–9g per kg body weight. Stick to low-fiber, white-grain sources (rice, white sourdough) to eliminate gastrointestinal stress.

3. Hydration & Electrolyte Strategy

Target 3.5L water daily + 1,500mg sodium / 400mg potassium. HYROX indoor arenas are warm; prevent late-race cramping on Station 7 (Lunges) & Station 8 (Wall Balls).

4. Recovery & Anti-Inflammatory Stack

Take 5g Creatine Monohydrate daily, 300mg Magnesium Glycinate before sleep for CNS recovery, and 2g Omega-3s to manage heavy eccentric lunge soreness.
